Aevorian Calendar — Support

Welcome to the official support page for Aevorian Calendar.

Aevorian Calendar combines a unique 13-month calendar system with the familiar Gregorian calendar, helping you organize appointments, explore dates, and experience time in a new way.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a traditional Gregorian calendar?

Yes. Open Settings and select Gregorian Calendar to use familiar months, dates, and weekdays.

Can I switch back to Aevorian?

Yes. You can switch between Aevorian and Gregorian calendar modes in Settings.

Does Aevorian support recurring appointments?

Yes. You can schedule daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly recurring events.

Can I use my existing Apple Calendar events?

Aevorian integrates with Apple Calendar, subject to the calendar permissions you grant on your device.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone:

Open the Settings app.

Tap your Apple Account name.

Select Subscriptions.

Choose Aevorian Calendar.

Select the available option to manage or cancel your subscription.

Subscriptions are managed through Apple.

What happens to my appointments if I cancel?

Appointments stored in your underlying Apple Calendar remain in Apple Calendar. Canceling an Aevorian subscription does not itself delete those appointments.
