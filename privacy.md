Aevorian Calendar — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 16, 2026

Aevorian Calendar respects your privacy. This policy explains how the app accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you use Aevorian Calendar on your iPhone or iPad.

1. Calendar Information

With your permission, Aevorian Calendar accesses calendars available through Apple's calendar system, including calendars connected to iCloud, Google, Outlook, and other supported accounts.

This access allows the app to display appointments, create and edit events, manage recurring events, and show upcoming activities in calendar widgets.

Calendar information is handled through Apple's calendar framework. Aevorian also stores certain information locally to support its own recurring-event functionality and widgets.

Your calendar accounts may separately synchronize information with their respective providers under those providers' privacy policies.

2. Location Information

Aevorian Calendar offers sunrise, sunset, and moon information.

You can choose to use your device's location or manually select a city. Location access is optional.

When you enable device location, the app uses geographic coordinates to calculate local astronomical information. Location information and your selected city may be saved on your device to support these features.

Apple's location and geocoding services may process location-related requests according to Apple's privacy practices.

You can change your location permissions through your device settings.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Aevorian Calendar stores information necessary for its features, which may include:

Calendar display settings and preferences.

Selected location or city information.

Recurring-event definitions and synchronization records.

Holiday preferences and downloaded holiday data.

Upcoming event names and times used by calendar widgets.

Some information is stored in an app storage area shared with Aevorian's widgets.

4. Sharing Calendar Information

Aevorian Calendar allows you to share a selected daily schedule using Apple's sharing features.

You can choose which events to include. The shared schedule contains the selected event names and times, rather than their notes or locations.

Information you choose to share is handled by the destination app or service according to its own privacy practices.

5. Internet Connections and Third-Party Services

Aevorian Calendar may use internet connectivity to retrieve updated holiday information from a GitHub-hosted resource.

When these requests occur, the service provider may receive standard technical information associated with an internet request, such as an IP address.

The app also relies on Apple system services for calendar access, location and geocoding, and other operating-system functions.

6. Subscriptions and Payments

If paid subscriptions are offered, purchases and subscription management are handled through Apple's App Store.

Apple processes payment and billing information under its own privacy practices. Aevorian Calendar does not need your complete payment-card information to provide App Store subscription access.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

You can change the app's settings and revoke calendar or location permissions through your device.

Uninstalling Aevorian Calendar generally removes its locally stored app data, subject to Apple's operating-system and backup behavior.

Events saved in your underlying Apple Calendar or connected calendar accounts are separate from the app. Uninstalling Aevorian Calendar does not itself delete those underlying calendar events.

Deleting an event or recurring series through the app, however, may change or remove events from the underlying calendar when you authorize that action.

8. Children's Privacy

Aevorian Calendar is a general-purpose calendar application and is not specifically directed toward children.

The app's calendar and location permissions are controlled through the device and its operating-system settings.

9. Security

Aevorian Calendar uses Apple's system frameworks and device storage facilities to support its features.

No method of electronic storage or internet communication can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app's features or privacy practices change. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
