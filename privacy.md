Aevorian Calendar — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026

Aevorian Calendar respects your privacy. This policy explains how Aevorian Calendar accesses, uses, stores, and shares information on supported iPhone, iPad, and Android devices. Some features differ by platform and app version.

1. Calendar Information

With your permission, Aevorian Calendar accesses calendar information available through your device's calendar system. On iPhone and iPad, this includes calendars made available through Apple's calendar framework, such as iCloud, Google, Outlook, and other connected accounts. On Android, the app uses Android calendar access and the calendars available on your device.

Calendar access supports features such as viewing appointments and upcoming activities, working with events and recurring events where supported, and displaying calendar information in widgets. The Android app requests calendar-read permission. Event creation or editing may use your device's calendar applications or services, depending on the feature and version.

Aevorian may store certain event-related information locally to support recurring-event functionality, synchronization records, and widgets. Your calendar accounts may independently synchronize events between devices through providers such as Google, Apple, or Microsoft, according to your account settings and those providers' privacy policies. Events appearing on two devices signed into the same calendar account do not, by themselves, mean that Aevorian shared events with another user.

Aevorian does not currently provide an Aevorian-operated cloud account or automatic calendar-sharing service between different Aevorian users. The app does not intentionally publish one user's calendar to other Aevorian users. However, calendar access and sharing settings configured in your device or calendar provider remain under your control.

2. Location Information

Aevorian Calendar offers sunrise, sunset, moon, and related astronomical information. Depending on the platform and version, you can use your device's approximate location or select a city manually. Location access is optional.

When enabled, location information may be used to calculate local astronomical information. Geographic coordinates or a selected city may be stored on your device to support these features. The device operating system or its location/geocoding services may process related requests under their own privacy practices. You can manage location permissions in your device settings.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Depending on your platform and enabled features, Aevorian may store:
- Calendar display settings and preferences.
- Selected city or location information.
- Recurring-event definitions and synchronization records.
- Holiday preferences or locally available holiday information.
- Upcoming event names and times for calendar widgets.

On iPhone and iPad, some information may be stored in an app storage area shared with Aevorian's widgets. On Android, the app uses local device storage and Android calendar services as applicable. Device backups and calendar-account synchronization are controlled separately by the operating system and account provider.

4. Sharing Calendar Information

Sharing is initiated by you. On iPhone and iPad, the daily-agenda sharing feature lets you choose events and share their names and times without their notes or locations. On Android, available sharing features may use the system share chooser to send selected calendar information or calendar-event data to another app. Depending on the sharing action, that data may include event details needed for a calendar entry.

Review what you are sharing and the destination before sending it. Once you choose a destination app or service, that recipient handles the information under its own privacy practices. Aevorian does not automatically share your calendar with unrelated Aevorian users.

5. Internet Connections and Third-Party Services

Some app versions may retrieve holiday information or other app resources from a GitHub-hosted resource. Internet services receiving such requests may receive standard connection information, such as an IP address.

Aevorian also relies on operating-system and third-party services as applicable, including Apple or Android calendar and location services, connected calendar providers, and the app store through which you obtain the app. Their processing is governed by their own privacy practices.

6. Subscriptions and Payments

On iPhone and iPad, subscriptions and purchases are managed through Apple's App Store. If subscriptions are introduced on Android, purchases will be managed through Google Play. The current Android testing version does not include completed Google Play subscription billing.

The applicable store processes payment and billing information under its own privacy practices. Aevorian does not require your full payment-card details to provide store-managed subscription access.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

You can change app settings and revoke calendar or location permissions through your device. Uninstalling Aevorian generally removes locally stored app data, subject to operating-system behavior and backups.

Events stored in an underlying device calendar or connected calendar account are separate from Aevorian's local app data. Uninstalling Aevorian does not itself delete those calendar events. Deleting or editing an event through a supported app feature or connected calendar service may, however, change that event in the underlying calendar.

8. Children's Privacy

Aevorian Calendar is a general-purpose calendar application and is not specifically directed toward children. Calendar and location permissions are managed through the device and operating-system settings.

9. Security

Aevorian uses platform calendar frameworks, permissions, and device storage facilities to support its features. No electronic storage or internet communication method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as app features or privacy practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
